Published evidence

A comparison of seven classifiers on this kind of data used an 80:20 split and reported XGBoost 96.0%, random forest 95.8% and SVM 94.84% accuracy, with online boarding and in-flight Wi-Fi as the service attributes most tied to satisfaction. Zenodo

A peer-reviewed data-mining study ranked online boarding, inflight wifi, baggage handling and inflight entertainment as the four services most worth improving. ScienceDirect (2021)

A community analysis of the same dataset found much higher satisfaction for Business class and business travellers than for Economy and personal travellers, with delays mattering less. This is not peer-reviewed, so treat it as supporting only. GitHub

Our own importance ranking agrees with these sources: digital touchpoints and travel context dominate.
